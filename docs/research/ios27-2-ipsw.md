# iOS 27.2 (24B119, iPhone19,2 / 19,3 / 19,7): can it be RAM-booted on the OnePlus 8 Pro? No (2026-10-29)

Asked for: prepare `iPhone19,2,iPhone19,3,iPhone19,7_27.2_24B119_Restore.ipsw` (in the workspace root, 13.7 GB) to boot like iOS 16.5, keeping the 16.5 installation, **RAM boot only, abort if the RAM is too small**. Aborted before touching the phone. All facts below are **observed** in the IPSW (`unzip -l`, `BuildManifest.plist`) or in this repository, except where marked *inferred*.

## Why it stops at RAM
- The phone has **8 GB** of RAM ([../devices/instantnoodle.md](../devices/instantnoodle.md)); the Android log shows model IN2023.
- The IPSW's operating system image `141-68953-104.dmg.aea` (BuildManifest "OS") is **9,529,458,688 bytes** already compressed; the system cryptex `141-68931-108.dmg.aea` 2,415,919,104; `141-68945-108.dmg.aea` 553,648,128; `141-68940-107.dmg.aea` 260,046,848; the app cryptex `141-08824-244.dmg` 16,777,216 and the restore ramdisk `141-10085-240.dmg` 245,366,811. Together about **12.7 GB** compressed, before the kernelcache (24 MB), the firmware and anything decompressed. Even the OS image by itself does not fit in 8 GB.
- The iOS 16.5 port's RAM boot does not hold the system either: the root volume and both cryptexes are read from the `darwin` partition of the flash (T40), the RAM carries a small image. Doing that for 27.2 would need the volume decrypted, written to flash and room for it; the existing 64 GB `darwin` partition holds the 16.5 volume, which was to be kept.

## What else would stop it (not tried, so *inferred* unless said)
- **The images are Apple Encrypted Archives** (`.dmg.aea`, observed): the key is fetched from Apple's servers per file (`aea` / `ipsw` tooling); nothing in this workspace decrypts them.
- **arm64e.** The three product types are A19-class (chip `t8160` in the firmware names, observed). iOS 26 and later userland is arm64e (pointer authentication, ARMv8.3); the Snapdragon 865's Cortex-A77 is ARMv8.2 and has no PAC: `retab`, `blraa`... are undefined there. The 16.5 port runs the **iPhone10,3 (A11, plain arm64)** userland for exactly that reason.
- **The kernel.** The kernelcaches (`kernelcache.release.v63`, `v64`, `v6x-pandia`, observed) are for Apple silicon (PPL/SPTM, exclaves: `exclavecore_bundle` in Firmware, observed). The port is built from open XNU 8796 (iOS 16.5); a 27.2 userland (new dyld, cryptex mounting, new syscalls and Mach interfaces, Secure Enclave and exclave services) needs a matching XNU whose source for this release was not looked for.
- The device-specific firmware in the IPSW (SEP, AOP, DCP, ISP, AGX, baseband `Mav25`) is all for the iPhone's chips and is of no use here.

## If it is ever wanted
A different goal would have to be chosen first: an iOS version whose userland is plain arm64 (the port's rule; iOS 16 is the last for A11) or a PAC emulation in the kernel's undefined-instruction handler for arm64e (slow and a project of its own), a flash root on a new partition (needs the owner's decision on the 16.5 data), and the AEA keys.

## Update 2026-10-29: the images were decrypted and inspected; erasing 16.5 was NOT done
Owner asked to erase 16.5 and bring 27.2 up on the 64 GB `darwin` partition, keeping all 27.2 images on the Mac and deleting nothing of 16.5. Result: **decrypting works, bring-up is blocked, so the phone and its 16.5 volume were left untouched** (*observed* unless marked).
- **AEA keys solved.** Each `.dmg.aea` header (profile 1, symmetric) holds `com.apple.wkms.fcs-response {enc-request, wrapped-key}`: an RFC 9180 **HPKE** message (base mode, DHKEM(P-256, HKDF-SHA256), HKDF-SHA256, AES-256-GCM, empty info and AAD; enc = enc-request, ciphertext = wrapped-key) sealed to the public key whose private half Apple publishes at the header's `com.apple.wkms.fcs-key-url` (`https://wkms-public.apple.com/fcs-keys/<id>`, fetch with `curl -L`; the same URL for all four archives; the id is base64url of SHA-256 of the public point). `ios272/fcs_key.py ARCHIVE PEM` prints the 32-byte key as base64; decrypt with `aea decrypt -i X.dmg.aea -o X.dmg -key-value "base64:<key>"` (the `base64:` prefix is required; a bare base64 or hex value gives "Error parsing key"). Scheme as in blacktop/ipsw `pkg/aea`.
- **Where the files are** (this Mac, `ios272/`, to keep for later reuse): the four `.dmg.aea` and their decrypted `.dmg` (OS `141-68953-104` 10.99 GB APFS container, system cryptex `141-68931-108` 6.2 GB, `141-68945-108` 650 MB, `141-68940-107` 260 MB), `keys/`, `Firmware/` (mtree, root_hash, trustcache). Nothing of `ios165/` or the 16.5 volume was touched.
- **The OS image mounts** (`hdiutil attach -readonly`): SystemVersion 27.2 / 24B119, `iPhone OS`; it has no dyld shared cache of its own (cryptexes carry it, `System/Cryptexes/{OS,App,ExclaveOS,Supplemental}`).
- **arm64e is real, not inferred.** `launchd`, `dyld` and `xpcproxy` have Mach-O cpusubtype `0x8000000c` (ptrauth ABI flag). `dyld` contains ~100 `retaa/retab` returns and 108 `paciasp/pacibsp/autiasp` hints; `launchd` has hundreds of authenticated branches (`blraa/braa`). `retab`/`braa` are UNDEFINED on the Cortex-A77 (ARMv8.2); `dyld` is the first userland code to run, so no process can start without either PAC emulation in the kernel's undefined-instruction handler or a rewritten userland.
- **Kernel still unsolved.** Open XNU 8796 (16.5) cannot host the 27.2 dyld, cryptex mounting, new syscalls and Mach interfaces; the shipped kernelcaches are Apple-silicon builds (PPL/SPTM, exclaves). This is the larger blocker.
- Consequence: erasing the 16.5 volume would give a 64 GB partition holding an OS that cannot run its first process, with no way back except rewriting 16.5 (`tools/darwin-write.py` from `ios165/rootfs.dmg`). Not done. To proceed, the owner would choose one of: (a) PAC-emulating undefined-instruction handler + a 27.2-capable XNU (research project, weeks), (b) an older arm64 (non-e) release, (c) staying on 16.5.

## Update 2026-11-02: the owner wants 27.2 installed over 16.5; what stands in the way, measured
Nothing on the phone was changed for this; 16.5 stays installed. The images were decrypted again into `ios272/` (git-ignored), with `ios272/fcs_key.py`.
- **This IPSW is the wrong build for this CPU, beyond PAC** [observed: masks over the executable mappings of all 78 caches, 1.15 G words; rough, a mask can hit data]. iPhone19's cache is `dyld_shared_cache_arm64e_x1`, tuned for the A19: PAC non-hint about 24 M (`blraa` 8.75 M, `pac*`/`aut*` by register 14.6 M, `braa` 885 k, `retab` 26 k, `ldraa` 10.6 k), and instructions a Cortex-A77 lacks that have no one-word equivalent: CSSC min/max 329 k, MOPS 30 k, `BC.cond` 224 k (this one is one word: clear bit 4), LRCPC2 90 k, SME 3.6 k, MTE 33 k.
- **A signed 27.2 (24B119) exists for older iPhones** [observed: api.ipsw.me]: iPhone12,1 11.0 GB, iPhone13,2 11.5 GB and later. Bergamota's census of iOS 26.7.1 for iPhone12,1 (A13) found a plain `dyld_shared_cache_arm64e` with LRCPC2 15, flag-manipulation 0, MTE 3859 on A19-only paths, and no SPTM, TXM or exclaves in its manifest [source: ry-diffusion/bergamota `docs/IOS26_FEASIBILITY.md`; for 26, not 27: re-measure on the 27.2 image].
- **The kernel exists** [source]: UniXNU (`patches/xnu-12377`, `profiles/os/ipados-27.0.1-24A446`, BSD-2-Clause-Patent) runs on `xnu-12377.121.6`, the newest published, with the Darwin 26 calls added (`darwin26-syscalls.patch`, `darwin26-shared-region.patch`); no Darwin 26 source is published. Its page says "In development", needs native PAC (SM8550), and says a device without PAC stays on arm64.
- **No PAC: rewrite the system** [source]: Bergamota (Poco F1, SDM845, no PAC, iOS 18 booted to SpringBoard) rewrites every arm64e Mach-O and the dyld cache (`tools/renegade`, `pacstrip*.py`), with a kernel fallback emulating non-hint PAC instructions. Its iOS 26 port on xnu-12377 was recorded as "built, nothing booted" on 2026-10-24. **The repository states no licence** (GitHub: `license: null`): read as reference, not copied here.
- **Backup of 16.5:** `ios165/rootfs.dmg` (`3.apfs`, 5628755968 bytes, at darwin block 0), `ios165/cryptex-os.dmg` (block 1374208), `ios165/cryptex-app.dmg` (block 2039808), written over adb from Android (TODO T40). Before an overwrite: an image of all of `darwin` from Android, through gzip, over `adb exec-out`.
- **The work, in order:** the iPhone12,1 IPSW; re-measure; port this repository's patch and drivers (`xnu/hackios.patch`, `xnu/files`, built for 8796) to 12377 plus UniXNU's Darwin 26 patches; a rewriter of our own; a root image; then backup, format, install. The kernel port is the largest part.
